PageRank Explorer

A random surfer follows a link with probability α and jumps to a random page otherwise. The PageRank vector r is the steady state: the eigenvector of the Google matrix G for λ = 1. Power iteration finds it by multiplying by G again and again. How fast it gets there depends on the second eigenvalue, |λ2(G)| = α·|λ2(M)|.

What to try

  1. Notes web: step to k = 5, 10 and 20 and compare with Table 2. Why is page D stuck at 0.0375 from the first step on?
  2. Move α to 0.5, then to 0.99. How do the ranking and the number of iterations change? Why does D shrink toward 0?
  3. Give D an incoming link: which pages lose rank? Did |λ2(M)| change?
  4. Two separate groups: why is |λ2(M)| exactly 1? Set α = 1. Why is there no single ranking any more?
  5. One-way cycle: at α = 1, start with all weight on A and press Play. Why does the iteration never settle, even though r exists?
